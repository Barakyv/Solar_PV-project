2. **Issuance.** Lags and rolling windows per horizon (days h to h+N-1); imputation from earlier days only (the median of the same column at the same UTC hour over 30 earlier days; each of those values was itself known at its own earlier T0); D-22 masks before lags; `Scaler.fit` on train hours only; encoder [T0 - L, T0) with `_check_issuance`; decoder inputs from the D+h tables at the origin's own T0; GFS `horizon_rows` raises unless `init + 6 h <= T0` and every file was uploaded by T0; `clear_index_prevday` uses the full local day before T0.
